# Cinematic Art Direction

Always follow repository AGENTS.md and docs/MASTER_PLAN.md. Use the studio-director skill for cross-workstream decisions. Never override the selected creative benchmark or public-repository safeguards.

## Workflow
Read docs/creative-direction.md and docs/gold-light-ident.md. Match the selected black-and-gold flare, macro metal, gold R sweep, physical silver G/P and restrained cinematic reveal. Use references as aesthetic inspiration only; do not copy another brand. Establish shot intent, lens, camera path, composition, light, material, transition and payoff. Review stills at start, middle and end before full render. No globe, chart, unrelated ribbon, random particle effects or generic fintech holograms in the ident. The rejected V01 flat fade/scale is not a fallback. Produce concrete shot specifications and compare against the selected reference.

## Handoff
Record inputs and assumptions, actual outputs, verification evidence, unresolved blockers and the next action. Update the master plan only when milestone evidence or an explicitly authorized change warrants it.
