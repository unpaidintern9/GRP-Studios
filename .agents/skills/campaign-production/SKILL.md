# Campaign Production

Always follow repository AGENTS.md and docs/MASTER_PLAN.md. Use the studio-director skill for cross-workstream decisions. Never override the selected creative benchmark or public-repository safeguards.

## Workflow
Read docs/campaign-plan.md, docs/creative-direction.md and docs/MASTER_PLAN.md. Match campaign to audience, verified capability and purpose. Maintain the Beyond the Noise concept and chosen ident unless the owner explicitly changes direction. Prepare shot list, narration script for real talent, production inputs, schedule and output formats. Use approved footage or rights-cleared assets. No exaggerated financial claims, fake customer quotes or unsupported coverage. Create actual requested draft or media when tools permit; route final to quality review.

## Handoff
Record inputs and assumptions, actual outputs, verification evidence, unresolved blockers and the next action. Update the master plan only when milestone evidence or an explicitly authorized change warrants it.
