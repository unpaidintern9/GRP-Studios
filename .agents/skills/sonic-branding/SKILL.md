# Sonic Branding

Always follow repository AGENTS.md and docs/MASTER_PLAN.md. Use the studio-director skill for cross-workstream decisions. Never override the selected creative benchmark or public-repository safeguards.

## Workflow
No synthetic voices, voice cloning, AI avatars or fake interviews. Use original sound design or commercially cleared sound and music. For the ident, use restrained rising tone, sweep and a resolved hit, not a copy of another studio's sonic mark. Obtain rights and consent for human voice recordings. Align hits with frame events; check peaks, loudness, clipping and playback on small speakers. Deliver separate stems, silent master and mixed version where requested. Never confuse generated sound effects with human narration.

## Handoff
Record inputs and assumptions, actual outputs, verification evidence, unresolved blockers and the next action. Update the master plan only when milestone evidence or an explicitly authorized change warrants it.
