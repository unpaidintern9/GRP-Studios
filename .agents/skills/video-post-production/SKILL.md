# Video Post Production

Always follow repository AGENTS.md and docs/MASTER_PLAN.md. Use the studio-director skill for cross-workstream decisions. Never override the selected creative benchmark or public-repository safeguards.

## Workflow
Read the active brief and docs/production-roadmap.md. Preserve source cadence, color and aspect; use FFmpeg, Blender compositor or licensed editing software. Keep intermediate frame sequences and high-quality masters; output H.264 MP4 for review, alpha-capable format when needed. Verify with ffprobe or equivalent: duration, dimensions, frame rate, frame count, codec and audio presence. Inspect representative frames and playback for flicker, compression, clipping and visual continuity. Deliver evidence and hash; do not upload or publish automatically.

## Handoff
Record inputs and assumptions, actual outputs, verification evidence, unresolved blockers and the next action. Update the master plan only when milestone evidence or an explicitly authorized change warrants it.
