# Campaign plan and storyboards

Baseline: November 24, 2026. Concepts for review; verify every product claim and actual interface before release.

## Flagship: Beyond the Noise

75–90 seconds (working cut: 85 seconds). Build a story about information overload and the discipline of looking deeper, then reveal GRP as a financial intelligence platform. Do not imply a guaranteed financial outcome.

| Time | Beat | Visual / audio direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | Noise | City streets, commuters, phones, conflicting headlines, layered human voices and ambient sound. |
| 10–20 s | The world behind markets | Markets, ports, factories, infrastructure and global commerce; real economy behind the numbers. |
| 20–28 s | Clarity | Everything slows; ambient noise falls away; original GRP signature sweeps across screen. |
| 28–42 s | Product reveal | Cinematic camera movement reveals the actual Terminal; approved real research pages, financial statements and charts. |
| 42–55 s | Connected intelligence | Choreographed equity research, market intelligence, ETF analysis and portfolio analytics, only where currently supported and approved. |
| 55–68 s | Human context | A believable financial professional at work; licensed or filmed footage, no fabricated testimonial or implied customer. |
| 68–80 s | Convergence | Product and brand come together; emblem resolves. |
| 80–85 s | Closing | Gilmer Research Partners, approved website address, signature sound and relevant disclosures. |

### Proposed human voiceover script

The world never stops talking.

Every headline. Every opinion. Every market move.

More information than ever before.

But information alone is not understanding.

Behind every number is a business.

Behind every business, a changing world.

And behind every investment decision, a question worth asking.

What matters?

We believe research begins with discipline.

With understanding risk.

With looking deeper.

Introducing the Gilmer Terminal.

Market intelligence. Investment research. Portfolio analysis.

One connected platform.

See beyond the noise.

Gilmer Research Partners.

### Delivery

Real human narrator, self-recorded if quality permits or a professional voice actor. Mature, understated documentary delivery, not an exaggerated trailer voice. No AI voice, voice cloning, avatars or fake interview audio. Record clean isolated narration; retain consent and commercial usage rights. Fit final duration to natural human pacing; review claims and disclosures before recording the final take.

## First proof of concept: 15-second silent teaser

No spoken narration; use professionally designed original/licensed music and sound effects.

- 0–3 s: compelling real-world or macro cinematic shot; sound establishes tension.
- 3–7 s: original GRP sweep transitions into accurate real product footage.
- 7–12 s: two or three product-detail moments with legible, intentional motion.
- 12–15 s: approved GRP wordmark, campaign line and closing sonic signature.

These beats are a proposed first editing sequence; choose shots after the ident direction is selected. The five-second ident is also delivered as an independent asset. No raw proprietary UI, unreleased data, sensitive customer information or official brand files in public Git.

Distribute approved assets across website, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, paid ads and advisor presentations. Deliver 16:9 master and deliberate 9:16, 1:1 and optionally 4:5 reframes. Keep text within safe areas and use readable captions for spoken versions. Publish only after brand, rights and financial marketing review.
