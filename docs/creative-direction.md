# Creative direction and reference films

Baseline: November 24, 2026. Working concepts, not approved final creative.

## Creative brief

Create premium GRP marketing with the production value of a major financial intelligence company and a recognizable entertainment-style identity. The owner wants cinematic, believable imagery, distinctive logo animation, and absolutely no fake voices. AI is permitted for production assistance, not at the expense of realism.

Combine Wealthsimple's emotional storytelling, Bloomberg's institutional credibility, Apple's precise product reveals, Prime Video's recognizable motion identity, and Robinhood Legend's bold modern product presentation. Borrow techniques rather than copying logos, protected designs, scripts, scenes or footage.

## Four complementary visual directions

- **Obsidian:** black brushed metal, architectural geometry, restrained luxury, carefully controlled highlights, tactile materials.
- **Intelligence:** precise luminous data lines, dimensional structures, genuine financial charts, sophisticated typography and meaningful motion.
- **The Real World:** authentic people, city streets, global commerce, ports, industry and infrastructure; naturalistic lenses, light and motion.
- **The Terminal:** dramatic, editorially composed actual product interfaces, clear charts, elegant device models and intentional camera moves.

Combine these four directions while retaining the existing official GRP identity. The dark obsidian look is a proposed film treatment, not authorization to replace official brand colors or logo geometry. Official brand assets are required for accurate production; no fabricated mark.

## GRP signature motion system

Produce three distinct concepts from official vector artwork and select a single visual language. Target a five-second master ident, like a studio opening rather than an animated web logo.

| Time | Beat | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 s | Emergence | Begin in darkness; a narrow light reveals the original emblem edge. |
| 1.0–2.5 s | Formation | The emblem's own geometry resolves as a physical dimensional structure; controlled camera movement and material reflections. |
| 2.5–3.5 s | Signature sweep | The mark or its geometric edge sweeps across the frame and becomes an elegant full-screen transition. |
| 3.5–5.0 s | Brand reveal | Approved GRP wordmark resolves; distinctive original sonic signature. |

Create additional variants from the same motion language: 1-second wipe, transparent overlay/alpha transition, closing sting, lower thirds, website loading animation, silent website version, and short/long logo holds. The signature sweep is inspired by the concept of Prime Video's branded transitions; design GRP's own movement and sound, without copying Prime's identity.

Use Blender or Cinema 4D with the original approved vector path; no generative redraws of logos or text. Preserve proportions, typography, clear space and legibility. Use a custom or appropriately licensed sound, not an imitation of the Prime or Netflix sonic mark.

## Tone and messaging

Understated, confident, modern, human, institutional without being sterile. Emotion and clarity first; restrained use of technical language. No guarantee of returns or suggestion of superior investment performance.

Preferred campaign line: **See beyond the noise.**

Alternates: Perspective changes everything. / Markets are complex. Research matters. / Intelligence with perspective. / Look deeper.

These are proposed slogans, subject to brand/legal clearance before publication.
