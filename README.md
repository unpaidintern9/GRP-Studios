# GRP Studios

Independent cinematic marketing, motion graphics, and video production toolkit for Gilmer Research Partners.

> **See beyond the noise.**

This repository preserves the creative and technical direction established on November 24, 2026. It is a planning baseline, not a claim that the studio, animations, or films are already built.

## Mission

Make GRP look and sound like a premium financial intelligence company: authentic cinematography, exceptional product motion, recognizable brand transitions, real human voices, and disciplined financial communication. Use AI selectively behind the scenes, never as a substitute for authentic people, accurate product imagery, or professional finishing.

## First deliverables

1. Three five-second GRP signature animation concepts based on approved original vector artwork.
2. A 15-second premium teaser, no narration, with cinematic footage, real product imagery, and original/licensed sound design.
3. A 30-second product launch film after visual direction approval.
4. A reusable motion/render toolkit and, later, a standalone studio interface.
5. A 75–90 second flagship film, **Beyond the Noise**, with real human narration.

## Isolation and ownership

This is intentionally separate from the Gilmer Research Terminal. No shared repository, database, credentials, runners, deployment hooks, or ingestion dependencies. Media and brand assets are kept outside Git until authorized and cleared for public release. The repository is initially public and may be transferred to the appropriate organization later. No open-source license is granted here.
